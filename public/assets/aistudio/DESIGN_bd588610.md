## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the warmth, richness, and culinary theatre of premium North Indian tandoor traditions and Indo-Chinese specialties. Built for an upscale dining and modern online ordering experience, the identity evokes the glow of clay ovens, fragrant spices, and late-night feast culture.

The visual style blends **Modern Tactile Opulence** with refined editorial restraint:
- **Atmospheric & Cinematic:** Deep, saturated burgundy and charcoal substrates convey intimacy and nocturnal warmth, reminiscent of ambient restaurant dining rooms lit by overhead brass fixtures.
- **Regal Warmth:** Warm gold metallic highlights and glowing ember accents punctuate menus, badges, and primary calls-to-action.
- **Culinary Clarity:** Clean structural layouts balance traditional warmth with modern digital efficiency, ensuring effortless navigation across rich imagery, dietary indicators, and customized orders.

## Colors
The palette evokes embers, spice jars, and molten gold against rich velvet backdrops. It operates natively in a rich dark mode to recreate cinematic restaurant dining ambiance.

- **Primary (`#7A1224`, Crimson Burgundy):** Represents signature gravies, clay tandoor hues, and brand authority. Accompanied by `#4A0D17` (Deep Wine) for elevated surfaces and `#2A060C` (Midnight Mahogany) for structural underlays.
- **Secondary (`#F4C430`, Warm Radiant Gold):** Used for focal interactive actions, featured tags, rating stars, and prominent banners. Supported by `#E5A93C` (Tandoor Ochre) and `#D4901C` (Antique Gold) for subtle outlines and hover interactions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#161214`, Smoked Charcoal):** Replaces harsh pure black with a warm, spice-tinted charcoal base. Elevated card containers leverage `#1F191B` and `#272023`.
- **Text & Contrast (`#FFF8EE`, Rich Cream / `#F5EBE1`, Soft Ivory):** High-contrast, warm typography replaces stark white to eliminate harsh glare on dark wine backgrounds.
- **Culinary Compliance Badges:**
  - *Vegetarian:* Forest Emerald (`#12803B`) border and central filled disc on a pale parchment or translucent background.
  - *Non-Vegetarian:* Crimson Ochre (`#A82218`) border and central filled triangle.

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs theatrical serif drama with pragmatic digital legibility:

- **Headlines & Display (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys regal grandeur, craftsmanship, and warmth. Display headings utilize italicized flourishes sparingly for spice categories and signature specialties.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and modern, preventing dish descriptions, ingredient customization, and nutritional notes from feeling cluttered.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Menu pricing adopts `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold weights to guarantee quick scanning across fast-paced ordering interactions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop with generous gutters, transitioning into a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of `1280px` centered with `margin` at `2.5rem`. Menu items reflow into 2 or 3 columns to balance appetizing food photography with item details.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 2-column food card presentation.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid with `margin-mobile` at `1rem`. Dish cards collapse into full-width horizontal cards featuring quick-add steppers and sticky order summaries.
- **Spacing Cadence:** Card padding utilizes `space-md` (`1rem`) internally, while cross-category spacing leverages `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to provide breathing room between heavy rich red tones.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical signage and warm backlit restaurant decor through low-key illumination rather than cold drop shadows:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Smoked Charcoal `#161214` with subtle radial vignette gradients bleeding to `#2A060C`.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Menu Blocks):** Deep Wine Red `#2A060C` or Midnight Plum `#1F191B` enclosed by hairline antique gold borders (`1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.2)`).
- **Layer 2 (Overlays & Dialogs):** `#4A0D17` with subtle golden glow diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px rgba(244, 196, 48, 0.08)`.
- **Gilded Borders & Filigree:** Decorative brackets, banner badges, and header ribbons use delicate gold framing (`1px` to `1.5px`) with corner notch styling to recall brass menu boards.

## Shapes
A balanced level 2 roundedness provides soft, welcoming corners (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline) without sacrificing the structure needed for opulent framing.

- **Buttons & Chips:** `8px` corner radius maintains an approachable modern tactile feel without collapsing into pure pills.
- **Decorative Ribbon Banners & Badges:** Distinct scalloped or bracketed silhouettes (bracket notches on banner containers) evoke vintage storefront signboards and ornamental brass plaques.
- **Food Images:** Soft `12px` (`rounded-lg`) curvature paired with subtle inner vignettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Golden Flame CTA):** Solid `#F4C430` background, dark charcoal text (`#161214`), `font-weight: 700`. Hover state transitions to `#E5A93C` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(244, 196, 48, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary (Wine Velvet):** Solid `#7A1224` background, cream text (`#FFF8EE`), with an antique gold border (`1px solid #D4901C`).
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.5)`, text in `#FFF8EE`, shifts to golden fill on hover.

### Food & Dish Cards
- **Structure:** Dark burgundy container (`#2A060C`), fine gold stroke (`1px solid rgba(244, 196, 48, 0.15)`), `padding: 1rem`.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Title in `Playfair Display` (`#FFF8EE`), spice indicator icons, dietary veg/non-veg mark in the top-right corner, description in ivory (`#F5EBE1` at 85% opacity), and gold price tag (`#F4C430`).
- **Add-to-Cart Stepper:** Outlined capsule with plus/minus toggles and gold active state.

### Dietary Standard Badges
- **Veg Badge:** `16px x 16px` square with `1.5px` border in `#12803B`, containing a centered filled circle (`7px` diameter) in `#12803B`.
- **Non-Veg Badge:** `16px x 16px` square with `1.5px` border in `#A82218`, containing an upward pointing equilateral triangle in `#A82218`.

### Category Chips & Filters
- **Default:** Background in `#1F191B`, text in `#F5EBE1`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 248, 238, 0.1)`.
- **Selected:** Background in `#7A1224`, text in `#F4C430`, border `1px solid #F4C430`, accompanied by a golden spark icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Dark charcoal fill (`#1F191B`), ivory input text, and golden ochre focus borders (`1.5px solid #F4C430`) with zero blue or cold undertones.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rich burgundy selected fill with gold checkmark/indicator dot.

### Special Offer Ribbons & Signage Badges
- **Heritage Banner:** Parchment or warm ivory slab background (`#FFF8EE`), deep burgundy text (`#4A0D17`), bracketed ornamental ends, and dual inner gold pin-striping (`1px solid #D4901C`).
